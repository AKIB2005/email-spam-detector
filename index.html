Email Spam Detector

Paste an email and the model will tell you whether it looks like spam.

The model runs inside your browser, so the email you paste is never sent anywhere.

About this model

ModelAccuracyReal emails wrongly flaggedSpam missed

Pipeline: clean the email (remove HTML, replace links, addresses and numbers with tokens) → TF-IDF features (words and word pairs) → linear classifier. Results are on a held-out 20% test set. The training data is the Enron email corpus, so the model is best at spotting classic spam and can be less sure about modern marketing emails.
