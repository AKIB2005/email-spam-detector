How HAMMER decided

Run a scan and this section fills in with the real numbers from your email.

1

Clean the email

HTML is stripped, then links, email addresses and numbers are replaced with placeholder tokens so the model learns patterns, not exact values.

2

Pick out the words that matter

Common words like "the" are dropped. Single words and word pairs are matched against the 20,000 features the model learned during training.

3

Add up the evidence

Every matched feature has a learned weight: positive pushes toward spam, negative toward legitimate. The weighted TF-IDF values are summed with a starting bias.

4

Turn it into a verdict

The score becomes a probability between 0% and 100%. Above the 50% decision line the email is called spam.
